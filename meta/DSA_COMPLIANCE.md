## App Transparency

1. **Content Moderation**: Not applicable - app contains no user-generated content
2. **Recommender Systems**: Not applicable - app does not use algorithmic recommendations
3. **Advertising**: Not applicable - app does not contain advertising
4. **Data Collection**: No personal profiles, analytics, or search queries are collected (species search runs 100% offline via SQLite FTS5/BM25; coarse region is cached locally)
5. **Third-Party Sharing**: No personal data is sold or shared for advertising or analytics. Static daily botanical imagery/metadata is fetched from GitHub Pages CDN, and optional In-App Purchases are processed by Apple App Store / Google Play Billing and synced anonymously via RevenueCat solely to verify entitlements.
